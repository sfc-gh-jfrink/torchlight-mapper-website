Support

Torchlight Mapper for iPhone and iPad

Email me: jkfrink@icloud.com
I am one person, not a support desk, but I read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Reporting a bug

The more of this you can include, the faster I can fix it:

  • What you were doing when it went wrong, step by step.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • Your device (for example “iPhone 15 Pro” or “iPad Air M2”) and your iOS version, from Settings → General → About.
  • The app version, shown on the app's home screen.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is visual.
  • If the problem is with a specific map, a shared .torchlight file of it. Only send one if you are comfortable sharing that map's contents with me.

Feature requests

Very welcome. Tell me the problem you hit at the table and I will figure out the feature. Real play situations are far more useful to me than a feature name.

Frequently asked questions

Is this app for players or for the person running the game?
For players. It was built for the person at the table who gets handed the graph paper and maps the caves, caverns, castles, and dungeons the party explores as they explore them. The person running the game can absolutely use it too, but that is not who it was designed around.
Does it work with the game system I play?
It should. Torchlight Mapper is a general-purpose grid mapper. It draws on a 5-foot square grid, which matches most dungeon-crawling systems, and it contains no rules, stat blocks, or publisher content of any kind. It is a mapping tool, not a rules companion.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
Neither. There is no sign-in and the app makes no network requests at all. It works fine in a basement with no reception, on a plane, or in airplane mode.
Where are my maps stored? Are they in the cloud?
On your device only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. If you have iOS device backups or iCloud Backup turned on, your maps may be included in that device backup, which is handled entirely by Apple.
How do I get a map to another player?
Export the dungeon as a .torchlight file and send it through the share sheet — Messages, AirDrop, Mail, Files, anything you have installed. When they tap the file, it opens in Torchlight Mapper. If they already have that same dungeon, the app shows a visual diff of what changed before they accept it.
Can I print a map or send a PDF to someone without the app?
Yes. Export as PDF or PNG, optionally including room notes and the adventure log, and print it via AirPrint or send the file anywhere.
I deleted a dungeon by mistake. Can I get it back?
Probably. Deleting a dungeon creates an automatic on-device backup that is kept for 30 days. Go to Settings → Backups and restore it. After 30 days the backup is removed automatically.
Can you recover my maps if I lose my phone or delete the app?
No, and I want to be very clear about this: I never receive your data, so I have no copy of it and no way to recover it. Deleting the app removes all of its maps and backups permanently. Export or share anything you would be upset to lose.
Why can't I move a room?
It is probably locked. Auto-placed rooms are locked by default so a stray drag pans the map instead of shifting your dungeon. Unlock it with the Lock toggle in the toolbar or from the room's info panel. Also note that an unlocked room has to be selected first: the first drag selects it, the second moves it.
The map spun sideways. How do I get north back?
A two-finger twist rotates the map. Tap the compass, or the recenter button that appears when the map is rotated, to animate back to north-up. Rotation is never saved.
Can I replay the tutorial?
Yes, it can be replayed on demand at any time; it is not a one-time first-run-only walkthrough.
Is it really free? Are there ads?
Free, with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscription.
